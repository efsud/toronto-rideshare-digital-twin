# Formative usability study — pending recruitment

Recruit five representative researchers/learners; record experience level, viewport and assistive technology with consent. Do not collect unnecessary personal information. Give a neutral introduction and ask participants to think aloud. Avoid teaching the answer during a task; log assistance separately.

1. Explain the boundary, hexagons, pickup/destination marks and driver states.
2. Distinguish an illustrative line from a road-following route and identify the active leg.
3. Explain one accepted/rejected offer and one queue cancellation using event evidence.
4. Compare P0 with another policy; interpret a paired difference and its uncertainty.
5. Find the model assumptions and identify what remains empirically unvalidated.

For each participant/task record completion without assistance, time, errors, quotation or observation, severity and proposed revision. Test keyboard-only navigation and map-table alternatives. Target at least four of five participants completing each core task without assistance, with no unresolved confusion between synthetic output and observed evidence. Revise failures and retest. This is a small formative sample, not a statistical certification.
